# DashLens Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

DashLens is an offer analyzer and mileage / earnings log for delivery drivers.

**What stays on your phone.** Everything. DashLens stores your mileage log, offers it has analysed, earnings, expenses, receipt photo references and settings in a private database on your device. DashLens has no internet permission, no account, no analytics, no ads and no servers. We cannot see your data and do not receive it.

**Accessibility Service.** To show the offer card and detect whether you are online or on an order, DashLens uses Android's Accessibility Service to read on-screen text of the DoorDash Dasher app (package `com.doordash.driverapp`) and, only if you switch on "Also read the Uber Driver app" in Settings, the Uber Driver app (package `com.ubercab.driver`). The Uber Driver app is named in the service's configuration so Android can deliver its screens to DashLens, but DashLens reads no text from it while that setting is off. It reads offer pay, distance, merchant name and status text. It never reads other apps, never taps, accepts, declines or types for you, and does not store personal information about customers.

**Screenshot reading (Uber, optional).** The Uber Driver app hides its offer card from Android's normal accessibility read. So, only while "Also read the Uber Driver app" is on and the setting "Read Uber offer cards from a screenshot" is on, DashLens takes a screenshot of your screen when the Uber Driver app is showing a screen it cannot read, and reads the text in it with a text-recognition model that is built into the app and runs on your phone. The screenshot is held in memory for that read only: it is never saved, never shared and never sent anywhere (DashLens has no internet permission). Only text that looks like an Uber offer is kept (as the diagnostic text in Settings); anything else that happens to be on screen is discarded. Turn the setting off to stop all screenshot reading.

**Location.** With your permission, DashLens records your GPS position while a shift is running to measure miles and to note where offers were accepted. Location is stored only on your device.

**Notifications, vibration, text-to-speech.** Used only for the shift notification, tax-date reminders and optional offer feedback.

**Photos.** Receipt photos you attach stay in your phone's gallery or the file you pick. DashLens stores only a reference to them.

**Sharing and export.** Nothing is shared unless you choose Share or Save in the app. Exported CSV files go to your Downloads/DashLens folder.

**Deleting your data.** Uninstalling DashLens deletes its database and settings. Receipt photos remain in your gallery until you delete them.

**Not affiliated.** DashLens is independent and is not affiliated with or endorsed by DoorDash or Uber. Earnings, mileage deduction and tax figures are estimates, not tax or financial advice.
