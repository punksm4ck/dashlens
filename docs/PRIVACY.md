# DashLens Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

DashLens is an offer analyzer and mileage / earnings log for delivery drivers.

**What stays on your phone.** Everything. DashLens stores your mileage log, offers it has analysed, earnings, expenses, receipt photo references and settings in a private database on your device. DashLens has no internet permission, no account, no analytics, no ads and no servers. We cannot see your data and do not receive it.

**Accessibility Service.** To show the offer card and detect whether you are online or on an order, DashLens uses Android's Accessibility Service to read on-screen text of the DoorDash Dasher app only (package `com.doordash.driverapp`). It reads offer pay, distance, merchant name and status text. It never reads other apps, never taps, accepts, declines or types for you, and does not store personal information about customers.

**Location.** With your permission, DashLens records your GPS position while a shift is running to measure miles and to note where offers were accepted. Location is stored only on your device.

**Notifications, vibration, text-to-speech.** Used only for the shift notification, tax-date reminders and optional offer feedback.

**Photos.** Receipt photos you attach stay in your phone's gallery or the file you pick. DashLens stores only a reference to them.

**Sharing and export.** Nothing is shared unless you choose Share or Save in the app. Exported CSV files go to your Downloads/DashLens folder.

**Deleting your data.** Uninstalling DashLens deletes its database and settings. Receipt photos remain in your gallery until you delete them.

**Not affiliated.** DashLens is independent and is not affiliated with or endorsed by DoorDash. Earnings, mileage deduction and tax figures are estimates, not tax or financial advice.
